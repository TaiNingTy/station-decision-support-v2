The project, in four parts

Real product ownership, demonstrated with public data.

Situation

Station configuration draws on planning, engineering and operations expertise. That knowledge lives in different documents and with different specialists, making consistent early-stage decisions difficult.

Task

I set out to turn that expertise into a repeatable decision-support product: interpret a site, propose a configuration, explain its basis and route unresolved questions to the right people.

Action

  • Structured spatial and demographic evidence into a shared station input.
  • Separated AI retrieval and interpretation from deterministic constraints and sizing.
  • Made scenarios, sources and human review part of the product experience.

Result

A working data-to-configuration demonstration across 21 stations, with three scenarios and traceable inputs. The next validation step is AI interpretation on these inputs and review of operational assumptions.
