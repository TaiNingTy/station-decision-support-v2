# KB_V2_04 · Open items, assumptions and decision ownership

Knowledge base document 04 of 06 · version kb-v2.1 · issued 2026-09-18 · cite as `KB_V2_04 §OI-xx (kb-v2.1)`

This document lists what the Miami demonstration has not acquired or has assumed, and who decides. The interpretation layer uses it to phrase questions for people; it never resolves an open item on its own.

## OI-01 Constructible platform space (assumption C06)

Not acquired for any station. Every module recommendation is conditional. Owner: the project owner with site or structural information. Rule: RC-07.

## OI-02 Platform module berth counts

M1 (6 berths) and M2 (7 berths) were read from the owner's drawings and are flagged for confirmation. Owner: the drawing owner.

## OI-03 Vehicle operating parameters (assumptions C01–C05)

Average party size 1.3, berth cycle 45 s, utilization 0.8, within-hour peaking 1.25, minimum headway 3 s are generic orders of magnitude, not supplier data; supplier operating data is deliberately not used in this public study. Owner: operations, with the supplier's confirmed product version.

## OI-04 Scenario assumptions (A01–A07)

Attendance 0.85, non-commute multipliers 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5, adoption 5 % / 15 % / 30 %, land-share allocation, PM mirror, departure-window proxy. Demonstration values. Owner: planning, with a calibrated service-choice study when one exists.

## OI-05 Renamed stations in the observed reference

Three report rows were matched by location because the reports use older names (Omni → Adrienne Arsht Center, Park West → Miami Worldcenter, Eighth Street → Brickell City Centre). Owner: the agency or the project owner. Rule: RC-09.

## OI-06 Origin-destination and hourly ridership

Not acquired. Without them there is no calibration and no peak-hour observation; the peak-hour share is a census proxy. Owner: the agency (data request).

## OI-07 Regional and hub inflow

Excluded from the scenarios by design; stations with external connections are understated. Owner: planning decides whether and how to model it. Rule: RC-12.

## OI-08 Walk catchment and entrances

The network walk model is not built; entrances, crossings, vertical circulation and barriers are not verified. Owner: planning and engineering with field verification. Rule: RC-13.

## OI-09 Engineering validation

Not performed. Nothing in the study is a design; feasibility, budget and the final configuration stay with people. Owner: engineering and the project owner.

## OI-10 AI run records

Every interpretation produced by the AI layer carries a run record (platform, mode, model, time, operator, review status). Only reviewed outputs are published; an unreviewed output is a draft.
